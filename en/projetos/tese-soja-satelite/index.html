PhD: crop prediction from satellite climate, filed as a patent application

My PhD research showed that satellite-measured climate variables, with no weather station, support forecasting municipal soybean production in Brazil's largest soybean region. UFU filed the method as a patent application.

Patent applicationBR 10 2025 019783 9 (INPI, owned by UFU), covering the thesis method

Where
UFU, PhD
Period
2019 - 2025
Role
Author of the research, the code and the thesis.

Project sheet

Task
Regression of municipal soybean production (tonnes) from satellite climate
Data and scale
34 seasons (1989 to 2022) in northern Mato Grosso, 24 FLDAS/NOAH variables and 216 seasonal features
Method and validation
Validation set held out from training, with RMSE, MAE and residual analysis
Result
Thesis approved on 20 Oct 2025 and method filed as patent application BR 10 2025 019783 9

Problem

Crop forecasting in Brazil depends on weather stations, which have gaps and sparse coverage exactly in the country's largest soybean region. The thesis question: can satellites replace the station?

Data and scale

  • Monthly FLDAS/NOAH series (NASA), downloaded automatically with file integrity logging.
  • 24 climate variables turned into 216 features per season, organized by the months of the soybean cycle.
  • Municipal production from IBGE/SIDRA, 1989 to 2022.

Approach

  • Per-municipality extraction of each variable from NetCDF (xarray).
  • Feature engineering by crop stage.
  • Gradient-boosted decision trees with z-score standardization.
  • SHAP interpretation and importance ranking by variable and by month.

How I validated it

  • Evaluation on a set held out from training, with RMSE, MAE and residual analysis.
  • Observed versus predicted per season.

Result

  • Water balance and radiation were the most decisive groups; the top variable was October subsurface runoff.
  • Thesis defended and approved on 20 Oct 2025.
  • UFU filed the method as patent application BR 10 2025 019783 9, with my advisor and me as co-inventors.

My role

Author of the research, the code and the thesis. Co-inventor on the patent application with my advisor.

Patent application BR 10 2025 019783 9

The application was filed on 16 September 2025, with the Federal University of Uberlândia as owner. Its registered title translates as "Method for forecasting agricultural yield through automatic selection of climate variables derived from remote sensing". It is a filed application, not a granted patent, and the technical content only becomes public once INPI publishes it.
