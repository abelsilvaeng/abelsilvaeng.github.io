Building segmentation on drone orthophotos with YOLO11

A roof segmentation network for drone orthophotos, trained on weak labels audited with LiDAR and tested on city blocks it never saw.

Inventoryof roofs and projected area per lot, generated automatically

Project sheet

Task
Instance segmentation (roofs) on a 6.5 cm orthophoto
Data and scale
Aerial survey of Lagoa Grande (Minas Gerais): 6.5 cm drone orthophoto and classified LiDAR
Method and validation
Block-level spatial split; acceptance threshold declared before training (mAP50 0.60, recall 0.65)
Result
Mask mAP50 0.78; precision 0.77; recall 0.69

Problem

An outdated urban cadastre hurts tax collection and public health planning. The municipality already had the aerial survey; the image still had to become a building inventory and projected roof area per lot.

Data and scale

  • Drone orthophoto at 6.5 cm resolution and a classified LiDAR point cloud from the same flight.
  • Normalized digital surface model (nDSM) derived from LiDAR, to separate what has building height.

Approach

  • Weak labels from the corrected classification, filtered by the LiDAR building class and by height; watershed to split attached houses.
  • Label audit before training: the classical method was dropped after inspection showed polygons on bare soil.
  • Fine-tuning a YOLO11 segmentation model (PyTorch) on orthophoto tiles.
  • Post-processing into an urban asset inventory and automatic computation of projected roof area per lot.

How I validated it

  • Block-level spatial split: the model is tested on whole city blocks absent from training, to reduce the effect of spatial autocorrelation on the metric.
  • Acceptance threshold fixed before training: mAP50 of 0.60 and recall of 0.65. The model passed both.

Result

  • Mask mAP50 of 0.78, precision of 0.77 and recall of 0.69 on the spatial validation.
  • Roof inventory and per-lot projected roof area sheet generated automatically.

My role

I built the training set, audited the labels, trained and validated the model and automated the inventory. Individual work, through GFX GEO.
