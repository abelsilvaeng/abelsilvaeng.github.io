Building detection in drone orthophotos with YOLO11

A neural network that finds every building in a drone orthophoto and recomputes built-up area lot by lot, using data from Lagoa Grande, Brazil.

0.78mask mAP50 on spatial validation; the acceptance threshold was 0.60

Where
GFX GEO
Period
2026
Role
Founder and engineer of record

Context

An outdated urban cadastre hurts both tax collection and public health planning. The aerial survey already existed; it had to become an inventory.

What I did

  • Built the training set from the hand-corrected classification, filtered by the LiDAR building class and by height, splitting semi-detached houses apart.
  • Fine-tuned a YOLO11 segmentation model with spatial validation, so the model is never tested on the blocks it trained on.
  • Produced an inventory of urban assets (buildings, pools, trees, lots and vacant land) with IDs that stay stable across flights.
  • Automated the spreadsheet that recomputes built-up area per lot.

How it works, in four stages

  1. Data

    • Drone orthophoto (6.5 cm)
    • Classified LiDAR

  2. Labels

    • Building class + nDSM
    • Watershed for semi-detached

  3. Model

    • YOLO11-seg
    • Spatial validation

  4. Output

    • Urban inventory
    • Built-up area per lot

Method

  • Weak labels audited before training: the classical method was dropped after inspection showed polygons on bare soil.
  • Acceptance criterion set before training (mAP50 of 0.60 and recall of 0.65).

Result

  • Mask mAP50 of 0.78, precision of 0.77 and recall of 0.69 on the spatial validation split.
