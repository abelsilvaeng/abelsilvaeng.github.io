Mentoring in data science and applied AI for business

A GFX GEO mentoring program on technology instrumentation for businesses: how to bring data science and artificial intelligence into a company, from raw data to a product someone actually uses to make decisions.

Format
Online via Google Meet, on Saturdays, with Abel Cruvinel

Who runs it

  • Engineer and PhD in Science and Technology (UFU).
  • Eight years of university teaching, 2015 to 2023, across 26 courses.
  • Senior data scientist serving Brazil's federal highway police, previously at DNIT and CODEMGE.
  • Founder of GFX GEO, an engineering firm focused on geospatial technology and data science.

The projects and the stack on this site are the mentoring's repertoire: spatial databases, APIs, predictive models, generative AI and automation, applied to real problems.
