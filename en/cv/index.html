Summary

Senior data scientist with more than ten years of turning geospatial, climate and operational data into products people use to make decisions. Currently serving the IT directorate of the Polícia Rodoviária Federal (Brazil's Federal Highway Police), where I authored APIGEO, the production geolocation API behind Projeto Mapear, and work on statistical audit models, a Databricks lakehouse and generative AI (RAG). Before that, I automated the official cartography of DNIT (the federal transport infrastructure agency) and ran the spatial database of Minas Gerais' state transport plan (PELT-MG). PhD in Science and Technology from UFU, with a patent application filed for a model that forecasts crop yield from satellite data alone. End-to-end profile: data engineering, modeling, APIs, AI agents, documentation and knowledge transfer, backed by eight years of university teaching.
