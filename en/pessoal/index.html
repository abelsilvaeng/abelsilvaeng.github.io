I was the one who explained things

I could see why my classmates struggled with the way a professor taught something, and I could teach it my way. That thread runs through everything: the school radio, the tutoring, the classroom, the mentoring, and the job I do today, which is turning data into an explanation.

It is a story of a lot of hard work and a lot of good people along the way. I am proud of it. It is also funny.
